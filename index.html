Better generative models, and better ways to guide them.

PRISM Lab is a research group in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Korea University, led by Hyungjin Chung. We build generative models and agents, and the guidance and alignment methods that let them reason, create, and act in real-world settings.

Research

Better generative models and agents give systems richer capabilities. Better guidance and alignment make those capabilities controllable, reliable, and useful.

More on our research

  • Generative AI and agents

    Efficient, coherent generation that does not rely on scale alone, including unified multimodal models and visual agents that understand, decompose, and create complex artifacts.

  • Video generation and world models

    Video models that are physically plausible, controllable, and able to predict how the world evolves: geometry, motion, interaction, and state.

  • Video understanding

    Representations and temporal reasoning that let models follow events coherently over time, rather than as a loose set of frames.

  • Inverse problems and computational imaging

    Theory and measurement-aware algorithms that use generative priors to recover signals from incomplete, noisy, or indirect measurements.

  • Medical AI

    Image reconstruction, report generation, segmentation, and digital therapeutics, pairing general-purpose models with domain knowledge.
